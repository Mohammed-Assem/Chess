Norway · Grandmaster

Magnus
Carlsen

A master of the endgame.

Magnus Carlsen became World Chess Champion in 2013. He held the classical world title until 2023, having defended it four times.

His games are a lesson in patience: small advantages, persistent pressure, and an extraordinary ability to find chances in seemingly quiet positions.

What to watch: how he keeps his pieces active and turns a small endgame advantage into a winning position.

Explore his story at the FIDE Open Chess Museum.

United States · International Master

Levy
Rozman

Making the game make sense.

Levy Rozman, known online as GothamChess, is an International Master, chess educator, and content creator.

Through game breakdowns, opening lessons, and entertaining commentary, he makes complicated positions easier to follow for people learning the game.

What to watch: how he explains mistakes, spots tactical opportunities, and connects each move to a clear plan.

Explore his lessons on the GothamChess YouTube channel.

You don’t need to be a master to enjoy the game.
